        ? Bare words match the prompt. key:value filters the numbers:
        seed:42 · steps:8 · cfg:0.5 · tcfg:1.5 · mu:none · model:flux · run:ab12cd · job:ab12cd · date:today · hash:3f9a · refine:12 · strength:0.4
        tcfg: is the scale the negative prompt ran at; hash: finds every image with the same pixels.
        refine: is the refine passes of that gallery image - refine:none the direct renders - and strength: how far they were re-denoised.
        id:12 is the image the page calls #12, and id:10-20 a span of them.
        run: is a grid however many times it was resumed; job: is one submission of it. date: takes today, yesterday or 2026-09-07 — a day on your clock, not UTC.
        Numbers also take a comparison or a range: cfg:>=1 · steps:8-12.
        mu:none is the images that ran with no shift at all — not the same as mu:0.
