          model:, quant:, lora:, run:, job: and hash: match part of the text; lora:none is the images made without a LoRA.

          Size ?The source is scaled to cover this size and cropped to its shape, never stretched. A pass may go past the 4 MP a direct render takes, to any 2 MP preset at 2×, since it keeps the source's composition. What fits on the card is the checkpoint's.

          Anime6B: ~18 MB author download on first use; tiled 4x upscale, then resized to the target. Sources: up to 4.19 MP and 4096 pixels per side. Quality, time and memory on this card are unmeasured.

          ?Strength: how much of the source is redrawn - low keeps the picture, 1 keeps nothing.

          Prompt and sampling

            Inpaint

            With the picture focused: B brush, E eraser, [ and ] size, I invert, Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y redo; the arrow keys move the brush and Space paints.

            ?How much of the masked part is redrawn - low keeps its picture, 1 keeps nothing of it. Outside the mask nothing changes. Several values, comma-separated, run side by side and open as a sheet.

            Prompt and sampling

            Outpaint

            ?How much of the new area and the band is redrawn. The new area starts as the server's fill, so a high strength is what is expected there. The original inside the band is kept pixel for pixel. Several values, comma-separated, run side by side and open as a sheet.

            Swap in

            Whole comma items match, whatever their case, spacing or underscores; a weight round the whole item is kept, and one typed in Find is ignored.               Refine

              Scale

              Each image keeps its own prompt, negative prompt, seed, CFG, mu and LoRA, and is refined from its own pixels. A reference image does not apply to a pass.

                More seeds

                A batch is the images of one run that differ only in their seed; picking one of its images takes all of it. Its new images keep its prompt, size and settings, take the seeds after its highest - any already made are stepped over - and run at the Batch it was made with, or the panel's Batch where that was not recorded. They join its run.

                  Gallery bundles

                  Save images, thumbnails and their gallery records in one ZIP file, optionally protected by a password: as a backup that keeps the gallery, or as a bundle of the entire gallery that then clears it. Import a bundle to merge its images with the current gallery.

                  Back up or bundle & clear

                  Bundle

                  Includes every model, every starred image and the recycle bin. The gallery's search and filters do not limit this action.

                  Reading the gallery…

                  Every bundle is saved on the server and verified by reading it back. Back up keeps the gallery as it is. Bundle & clear then removes the originals and old database, and creates a new empty database so you can keep generating. You can download the bundle afterwards.

                  With a password the bundle is encrypted as a .ldbundle file that only Local Diffusion can open. The password is not saved anywhere and cannot be recovered; without it the bundle cannot be opened, and a cleared gallery is lost.

                  Import a file

                  Existing images stay. Images already imported from this bundle are kept without creating duplicates.
